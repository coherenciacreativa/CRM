only a welcome-audio or MailerLite workflow. It should progressively combine
approved Instagram signals, email engagement and replies, manual evidence, and
future adapters; maintain identity, dedupe, provenance, and signal strength;

next-best-action previews, Fact Store, ledgers, scoring, and policy-bound Mati
assistance.

Weak signals must never become unauthorized outreach. Technical group mutation
must never be presented as real journey activation without observable trigger
and delivery evidence.
