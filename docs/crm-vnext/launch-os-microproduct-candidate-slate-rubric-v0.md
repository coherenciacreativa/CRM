## Evidence Rules

- Mark explicit canon as `source_explicit`.
- Mark inferred fit from multiple sources as `source_implied`.
- Mark weak or unverified concepts as `hypothesis`.
- Influences not found explicitly in Brand Hub, including Alan Watts, Seligman,
  Satyananda, neuroscience and therapy, must be labeled `Alejandro candidate
  input / hypothesis`, not Brand canon.
- Do not treat seed tests, internal QA, Null Audience work or local rehearsal as
  market evidence.
- Do not infer private CRM observed events.

## Gate Criteria

### 1. Brand Fit

Green:

- Feels clear, warm, intelligent and useful for deciding with criterion.
- Orients before pushing.
- Uses grounded spiritual seriousness without mystical cliche.
- Avoids generic funnel language in public copy.

Yellow:

- Fits the theme but needs Alejandro voice pass.
- Public promise is useful but still sounds like a template.
- Concept depends on a weak source or unconfirmed lineage.

Red:

- Uses pressure, manipulation, exaggerated certainty or magical promises.
- Introduces a new positioning frame without explicit decision.

### 2. Customer Value Promise

Green:

- The person receives something useful even if they never buy.
- The promise is small, honest and concrete.
- The value can be delivered in one interaction or a short sequence.

Yellow:

- Value is plausible but broad.
- Needs sharper "for whom / what change / what remains outside" definition.

Red:

- Promise depends on diagnosis, cure, guaranteed outcome, hidden upsell or
  unrealistic transformation.

### 3. Product Value Gate

Green:

- Can be reviewed local-only before any live boundary.
- Can be tested with internal/seed rehearsal without pretending market signal.
- Has a clear next human/product decision.

Yellow:

- Useful but needs better format, asset or funnel scope.
- Learning question is not yet crisp.

Red:

- Requires live audience, CRM writes, Shopify publish or MailerLite workflow
  activation to know whether it has value.

### 4. Ethical / Claims Safety

Green:

- Avoids absolutes and unverifiable claims.
- Avoids therapy, diagnosis, cure or medical/clinical framing unless explicitly
  approved and supported.
- Names uncertainty honestly.

Yellow:

- Touches sensitive psychological/spiritual territory and needs tighter wording.

Red:

- Implies treatment, diagnosis, guaranteed relief, superiority or authority not
  supported by existing evidence.

### 5. CRM Learning Potential

Green:

- Produces ethical learning signals such as topic interest, selected route,
  content receipt, qualitative reply prompt or preferred next depth.
- CRM write readiness remains deferred until real private observed events exist.

Yellow:

- Learning is useful but not clearly separable from delivery.

Red:

- Requires private observed events that do not exist or would turn seed/internal
  QA into market evidence.

### 6. Funnel Feasibility

Green:

- Can follow: Instagram post/video -> link -> microproduct -> email capture ->
  delivery/receipt.
- Can be rehearsed with preview/local artifacts and Null Audience/seed test
  posture if later approved.

Yellow:

- Requires more assets, Shopify preview work or MailerLite structure before CEO
  review.

Red:

- Requires public audience send, live workflow, live Shopify change, subscriber
  mutation or CRM write as the first test.

### 7. Asset Complexity

Low:

- One page, one email, checklist, short worksheet, simple guide.

Medium:

- Quiz logic, small interactive/game, short email course, audio plus transcript.

High:

- Rich media, custom game mechanics, multi-step automation, polished audio
  production or multi-channel campaign.

### 8. Format Suitability

Use:

- `guide` when the value is orientation or explanation.
- `quiz` when the value is self-recognition or segmentation by chosen path.
- `game` when a lightweight interaction can reveal preference without trivializing
  the topic.
- `audio` when embodied pacing, pause or practice is the value.
- `email_course` when the value unfolds over several editorial touches.
- `checklist` when the value is decision support or readiness.
- `worksheet` when the value is reflection that benefits from writing.
- `hybrid` only when combining formats reduces friction or improves value.

## Output Recommendation Bands

- `priority_a`: strong Brand fit, useful value promise, low/medium complexity,
  clear learning, small responsible test path.
- `priority_b`: promising but needs sharper CEO/product decision.
- `hold`: weak evidence, risky claim surface, high complexity or unclear value.
- `reject_for_now`: requires live mutation, invented evidence or unsafe claims.

## Safety Boundary

This rubric authorizes only local evaluation and CEO-facing recommendation. It
does not authorize live APIs, MailerLite drafts, Shopify live changes, CRM
writes, sends, subscriber/group/workflow/campaign mutations, ledgers, cards,
scoring, Fact Store writes, CRM Core work or Brand Hub patches.
